The story so far

It started with clay.

I grew up speaking four languages and thinking across three cultures. It's probably why I became a marketer. The same message can mean everything to one person and nothing to the next. That gap is where I work.

01

Jainam Ceramics · Belagavi

It started with clay.

My family makes ceramics. I learned business the honest way: a real product, real customers, nobody to hide behind. I led our move online and sales grew 15%.

Lesson one: marketing is mostly showing up, listening, and fixing what's broken.

02

WLDD · Founding hire · Bangalore

Then, Bangalore.

A brand-new LinkedIn creator vertical. No team, no playbook. I built a 300-creator network with 95% retention, talked 1M-follower creators down to £250–300 a post without burning a bridge, held a 65% margin, rebuilt workflows (+50% throughput) and grew it into a 10-person team.

15+ campaigns for Upstox, Max Fashion, Vantara and Damensch, activations for Flipkart and Zepto, and launches for Mamaearth and Minimalist.

03

Warwick Business School

Then, a one-way ticket.

MSc Management. Distinction, top 10%, merit scholarship. I supported myself in a country where I knew nobody, and learned British culture the fastest way possible: pouring pints.

best crash course there is.

04

Elle's Coventry & The Almanack

Then, a marketing department out of thin air.

A multi-brand hospitality launch with zero marketing infrastructure. I built it across 11 channels and 6 brands. Email opens 19% → 47%. A 67,925-contact B2B campaign at 46% opens. 100+ influencers pitched, 50+ at the launch, for free.

05

Speed Communications · PR

Then, the chase.

Revlon and Protexin pitches. A Telegraph journalist replied in week three. That's when I realised I love the chase.

06

Pahli Hill Bandra Bhai · Fitzrovia

Now: London, and a lot of phone calls.

Business development for a Michelin Bib Gourmand restaurant. 50+ cold calls a day to central London's 4- and 5-star hotels. Three weeks: 30 qualified leads, £4K recurring pipeline, £2K booked.

I cut £1K/month of dead ad spend and built an AI agent that sends 150+ personalised emails a day. Partnership channels: 1 → 6.
